JASSAN Technologies & Electrical Services (JTES) respects the privacy of our clients, customers, students, partners, and website visitors. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

1.Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your name, email address, telephone number, institution, project details, order information, feedback, and other information you voluntarily provide through our website, forms, Project Space, training registrations, or service requests.

2.How We Use Your Information

We use this information to provide our services, process orders and payments, manage projects, communicate with clients, provide technical support, and improve our services.

3.How We Share Information

JTES does not sell your personal information. Information may only be shared with trusted service providers where necessary for payment processing, delivery, website operation, or where required by law.

4.How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect personal and project information.
